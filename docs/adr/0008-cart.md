# ADR-0008: A small cart, several items per order

- Status: Proposed
- Date: 2026-11-24
- Decided by: (pending, Klaus)

## Context

Shipping is charged per order. A postcard alone pays more for shipping than for the card; customers buying a mug and a print should get one shipment.

## Options considered

1. **Cart** keyed by a cookie, server-side rows, items from any picture, expires after 30 days.
2. **Buy now**, one item per order: simpler, but worse for small items and for anyone buying two things.

## Decision

Option 1.

## Consequences

- A cart page and quantity edits; the cookie is functional (no consent banner needed for it).
