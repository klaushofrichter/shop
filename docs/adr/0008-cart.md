# ADR-0008: Several products of one picture per order

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-11-24
- Decided by: Klaus, 2026-11-24: "selects there one or more products for this image"

## Context

Shipping is charged per order. A postcard alone pays more for shipping than for the card; customers buying a mug and a print should get one shipment.

## Options considered

1. **Cart** keyed by a cookie, server-side rows, items from any picture, expires after 30 days.
2. **Buy now**, one item per order: simpler, but worse for small items and for anyone buying two things.
3. **Several products of one picture** per order (a cart scoped to one picture).

## Decision

Option 3 (amended 2026-11-24 from the proposed option 1, following Klaus's description of the process). A customer who wants products of two pictures places two orders. The schema keeps the picture per order item, so mixing pictures can be allowed later without a migration.

## Consequences

- A cart page and quantity edits; the cookie is functional (no consent banner needed for it).
